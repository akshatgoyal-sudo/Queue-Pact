# Scheduling evaluation protocol (frozen before candidate evaluation)

Development seeds 100–109; held-out seeds 200–209. Ten equally weighted families, 40 base students each: light (.3/min), normal (1.2/min), heavy (3/min), burst (batch), verification bottleneck (1/2/2/1 desks), photo bottleneck (3/1/2/1 desks with photo 3–6 min), payment bottleneck (2/2/1/1 desks with payment 4–7 min), missing-heavy (80%), closure (V1 closed 10–25), slowdown (verification 2x from 10–30). All other settings are original defaults. Surges/preset payloads generated once, shared across policies.

Primary objective: equal-case mean completed-student queue wait. Incomplete runs never qualify for ranking. Compare Ordinary, original v1 and one final v2 on held-out cases only after freezing candidate parameters. Development candidates: prerequisite head-start with photo penalty 1 or 2 times the critical service mean; preparation is immediate when eligible. Policy uses configured means and elapsed service, never future inputs. Candidates share FCFS plus existing fair-age protection.

Release gate fixed in advance: all held-out runs complete; v2 mean wait at least 5% below v1 and no higher than Ordinary; mean p90 no higher than either reference; no family mean wait or p90 more than 5% worse than either reference. Otherwise experimental, existing defaults unchanged. Accept individual regressions only with explicit disclosure, even if gate passes. Win/tie/loss tolerance 0.01 minutes. Worst regressions reported per case. No student-level uncertainty intervals; cases weighted equally, hence each family equally. Runtime includes engine construction and completion, excludes output serialization. No tuning after held-out evaluation.

## Candidate freeze after development, before held-out evaluation

400 development runs completed in about 8 seconds. Equal-case mean waits: Ordinary 37.5942, v1 41.1620, coefficient 1 candidate 42.4300, coefficient 2 candidate 42.7883 minutes. Neither candidate establishes an overall improvement. Select coefficient 1 only for the predeclared held-out evaluation because it has the lower development mean and p90 among the two candidates. Freeze algorithm/configured-mean estimator and coefficient 1 now; no further tuning. The anticipated release is experimental unless the predeclared gate is met. Do not reinterpret a comparison with v1 as superiority to Ordinary.
